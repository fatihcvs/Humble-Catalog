Contrast is measured with the WCAG relative-luminance formula against the two tab backgrounds a favicon actually sits on: white for light chrome, #282b31 for dark. 3.0 is the floor (WCAG 1.4.11, non-text contrast); below it a spine starts dissolving into the tab strip. The dark fill must also be lighter than the light fill — that is the whole point of having two palettes.
